About Remote Job Finder

Remote Job Finder collects fresh remote jobs and internships in every field and filters them for people based in India. Listings are fetched live from the job sources whenever you open the site. Each source is re-checked as often as its rules allow (from every 15 minutes to every 6 hours), and the page picks up new jobs every 5 minutes while it stays open.

Where the jobs come from

Listings come from the public job feeds of Remotive, Remote OK, Jobicy and Himalayas. Every listing shows its source, and the Apply button opens the original posting on that site. This site doesn't host, edit or verify the jobs.

How "Open to India" works

A job counts as open to India when its location says India, worldwide, anywhere or APAC. Jobs that don't state a location are shown with a "Check location" label. Jobs limited to other countries, or asking for US/UK/EU work authorisation, are hidden unless you choose "All locations". Always confirm on the original posting.

Your privacy

  • No accounts, no sign-up and no tracking cookies.
  • Saved jobs, applied jobs and your last-visit time are stored only in your own browser (local storage). Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Your searches are run in your browser and are not sent to us.

Stay safe

Never pay to apply for a job, buy "training" to get hired, or share bank details before you have a real written offer. If a listing looks like a scam, report it to the source site it came from.
